Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the expense data you choose to log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions of your transactions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service — authenticating your account, storing your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third-party services. Where the Service relies on external providers for functionality such as font delivery, only technical information necessary to load those resources (such as your browser type) is shared, and never your financial data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
